      Ranking criteria

      Applied in order below, only when players are tied on every rule above it.

      1. WITHDRAWN — players who withdrew Withdrawn players are ranked lower than all others, before any other rule is considered.
      2. WINS — total wins Higher total wins ranks first.
      3. DIFF — overall score differential Higher across all games ranks first.
      4. PLAYED — games played Fewer games played ranks first.
      5. P2P WINS — head-to-head wins within the tied group Only games played against others still tied at this point are counted.
      6. P2P DIFF — head-to-head score differential within the tied group Same head-to-head games as above, compared by score difference.
      7. PICK — draw pick number Lower pick number ranks first; always resolves any remaining tie.

      All matches must have a winner to unlock the playoffs.

      1 About

      This badminton manager tool helps you easily organize badminton events by managing players and courts, and automatically generating schedules based on your parameters.

      This tool was created to provide a generator that builds randomized matches while still following specific rules, such as keeping teams balanced in skill.

      Of course, this is not strictly designed for badminton matches. Feel free to use it for any games that are played in doubles.

      2 Beyond Badminton

      This tool was originally created for "Beyond Badminton", a community of badminton enthusiasts who organize events and tournaments.

      3 Privacy & Data

      Built with privacy in mind, this tool runs entirely locally on your device using JavaScript. All data is saved directly to your browser's local storage. We do not use cookies or upload any information to external servers.

      You can clear your saved data at any time using the built-in cleanup buttons or through your browser's privacy settings.

      To access your application data on a different browser or device, you can use the Export and Import features.

      4 How it works

      4.1 Players tab

      First, add players to the "All Players" list. This serves as your permanent saved roster for future tournaments.

      Then, select which players will be active for the current tournament and set their arrival times and play durations.

      4.2 Courts tab

      Next, add your courts to the Courts list, which also saves permanently for future use.

      After that, set the court availability by specifying the start time, duration, and which specific courts are open during that time block.

      4.3 Matches tab

      Finally, go to the "Matches" tab. Here you can generate trainings and tournaments.

      Training sub-tab

      The "Training" sub-tab allows you generate a training schedule based on your active players and available courts. You can fine-tune the results by adjusting the number of matches per hour, allowing singles matches, and more.

      The generated schedule displays all matches, player stats, and penalty scores. It also supports drag-and-drop, allowing you to easily rearrange matches while keeping an eye on how penalty scores change.

      You can print or export the schedule to share it or bring it with you to the courts. If you enter the scores in the tool, they will be printed; otherwise, the score fields will be left blank so you can write them in as you play.

      Tournament sub-tab

      The "Tournament" sub-tab allows you to generate a fully randomized, anonymous tournament schedule, automatically substituting placeholder numbers with player names.

      Tournament UI streamlines competition management from start to finish. Our step-by-step system guides you smoothly through every phase of your event—from conducting the initial draw and generating schedules to tracking match results and running the final playoffs.
