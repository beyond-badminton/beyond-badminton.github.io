          1. About

          This badminton manager tool helps you easily organize badminton tournaments by managing players and courts, and automatically generating schedules based on your parameters.

          This tool was created to provide a generator that builds randomized matches while still following specific rules, such as keeping teams balanced in skill.

          Of course, this is not strictly designed for badminton matches. Feel free to use it for any games that are played in doubles.

          2. Beyond Badminton

          This tool was originally created for "Beyond Badminton", a community of badminton enthusiasts who organize events and tournaments.

          3. Privacy & Data

          Built with privacy in mind, this tool runs entirely locally on your device using JavaScript. All data is saved directly to your browser's local storage. We do not use cookies or upload any information to external servers.

          You can clear your saved data at any time using the built-in cleanup buttons or through your browser's privacy settings.

          To access your tournament data on a different browser or device, you can use the Export and Import features.

          4. How it works

          Players tab

          First, add players to the "All Players" list. This serves as your permanent saved roster for future tournaments.

          Then, select which players will be active for the current tournament and set their arrival times and play durations.

          Courts tab

          Next, add your courts to the Courts list, which also saves permanently for future use.

          After that, set the court availability by specifying the start time, duration, and which specific courts are open during that time block.

          Schedule tab

          Finally, go to the Schedule tab and click Generate. The tool will create a schedule based on your active players and available courts. You can fine-tune the results by adjusting the number of matches per hour, allowing singles matches, and choosing whether players with only 1 hour of playtime can sit on the bench.

          The generated schedule displays all matches, player stats, and penalty scores. You can easily print or export the schedule for sharing.
